Lumbini

The birthplace of Siddhartha Gautama, the Buddha. A sacred garden of peace in Nepal and a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Explore Lumbini

Where the Buddha was born

Lumbini is in Rupandehi District, Lumbini Province, in the Terai plains of southern Nepal. Buddhist tradition says Queen Maya Devi gave birth to Prince Siddhartha here, under a sal tree, more than 2,500 years ago.

Today millions of pilgrims and visitors come to walk through the Sacred Garden, see the ancient ruins, and visit monasteries built by many countries.

Main attractions

A few of the most important places to see in Lumbini.

Maya Devi Temple

The most sacred spot, marking the place of the Buddha's birth.

Ashoka Pillar

A stone pillar raised by Emperor Ashoka in 249 BCE.

World Peace Pagoda

A white stupa built by Japanese Buddhists, a symbol of world peace.

Quick facts

  • Location: Rupandehi District, Lumbini Province, Nepal
  • UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1997
  • Sacred to Buddhists around the world
  • Main festival: Buddha Jayanti (Buddha Purnima), in April or May
